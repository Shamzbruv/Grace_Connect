Grace Connect 1.0.30-beta+32

- Fixed auto-attendance marking members "late" when they were genuinely on time. Both the automatic geofence check-in and manual sign-in graded lateness against the moment the record was written instead of the moment the member actually arrived (already tracked internally, just never used for this) -- a delayed background check-in, or a member who had to fall back to manual sign-in, could get flagged late despite being on site the whole time.
- Fixed a misleading "check your connection" error shown during manual check-in. The real cause was GPS struggling to get a fix indoors (common inside a church building), not a network issue. Location lookup now falls back through medium and reduced accuracy instead of giving up after one high-accuracy attempt, and shows an honest, accurate message when it still can't get a fix.
- Fixed iOS auto-attendance silently requiring less than it actually needs: the app only ever enforced "Always" location access on Android, even though iOS's monitoring mechanism also needs it to survive the app backgrounding. iOS could previously "start monitoring" on While-Using-App access and then go quiet the moment the phone locked.
- Battery optimization silently blocking background auto-attendance is now shown directly on the Attendance screen with a one-tap fix, instead of only on a separate settings screen nobody thinks to check when it seems to have stopped working for no reason.
- The first-time auto-attendance prompt now explains why the permission is needed, gives real step-by-step instructions for both Android and iOS, and clearly states the consequence of declining (manual sign-in every service) -- declining used to do nothing visible at all.
- Added real charted attendance analytics for pastors/admins: a stacked present/late/remote/absent breakdown, an attendance-rate trend line, and an accessible data table, backed by the server-side per-service attendance closeout. Also fixed the church dashboard's "weekly attendance" chart, which was plotting three hardcoded zero points padded in front of two real numbers.
- Added customizable, branded sharing for the Daily Word and Bible verses: choose from 12 curated backgrounds, adjust blur and darkness, pick a font and text color, and share -- every card carries the Grace Connect watermark. Scripture sharing previously shared plain text only.
